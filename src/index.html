About this unofficial Tibia tool

This site reads pasted hunting session logs and estimates Bestiary or task progress. It is not an official profile mirror and its projections can differ from in-game results.

Creature and achievement metadata comes from TibiaDraptor; map areas from Tibiopedia.pl. Tibia is property of CipSoft GmbH.
